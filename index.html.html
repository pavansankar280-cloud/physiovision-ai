Biomechanical Mathematics & Physics Guide

This project computes body angles in 2D vector space directly from normalized landmark coordinates $(x, y)$ generated by MediaPipe Pose deep learning network.

1. Joint Angle Calculation (Inverse Tangent Method)

Given three key joints $A(x_1, y_1)$, $B(x_2, y_2)$ [Vertex], and $C(x_3, y_3)$:

$$\theta = \left| \arctan2(y_C - y_B, x_C - x_B) - \arctan2(y_A - y_B, x_A - x_B) \right| \times \frac{180}{\pi}$$

If $\theta > 180^\circ$, then $\theta_{final} = 360^\circ - \theta$.

2. Cervical Spine (Neck Tilt) Vector Formula

Calculates the angle between the Ear-Shoulder vector and the true vertical axis $[0, -1]$.

$$\theta_{neck} = \arccos\left( \frac{\vec{v}_{ear\rightarrow shoulder} \cdot \vec{v}_{vertical}}{\|\vec{v}_{ear\rightarrow shoulder}\|} \right)$$
3. Clinical Application in Ergonomics

For every 1 inch the head projects forward, the cervical spine experiences an additional 10 lbs of force. PhysioVision AI detects forward-head tilt when the cervical vector exceeds 25° relative to neutral calibration.
